### An Engineering Student Fighting with Blueprints, Flies to Japan

Majoring in mechanical engineering at university, whenever my friends joked that I was a "future CEO," I shouted inwardly, 'Hmph, I am the body that will rule the world with blueprints.' And with that dream, I studied abroad in Japan, earned a master's degree, and walked a somewhat elite course.
After graduation, I got a job at a large Japanese company with high spirits. My role was to design and improve production lines and create a 'more perfect factory.' Like a robot controlling robots, I focused all my nerves on perfect movement without errors. But even I, who was called the factory ace, had one worry. "How can I analyze this vast amount of data faster and more accurately?"
That's when Python caught my eye. As I created programs to process and analyze data using Python, I slowly realized. 'Ah, this is like magic.' When I made a web app so that my colleagues on site could see this data at a glance, I still can't forget their eyes. Perhaps I was already addicted to the taste of coding at that moment.

### Life Patch Started After Becoming a Dad!

A very important 'patch' was updated in my life, which was sailing smoothly. It was the birth of my first child. I, who used to fly around the field, now came to cherish the time with my child more. Naturally, working on site became burdensome, and I needed a new breakthrough.
Just then, a good opportunity came. A DX boom was happening within the company, and coinciding with that, the IT department was also recruiting people within the company who could do IT. When I expressed my desire to move to that department, my boss was surprised and opposed it tremendously, but after twists and turns, I was finally able to persuade him. Yes, that's how I left the production line and moved to the IT department with full air conditioning. My colleagues around me envied me, saying, "You turned your life around with an internal transfer from factory work!" (Actually, I was shouting 'Nice!' inside.)

### Full Stack Junior, Finally Coming Out to the World!

New challenges were waiting for me in the IT department. In the new department where I only knew Python, through a bootcamp, I entered the path of a full-stack developer, literally learning **JavaScript, TypeScript, React, SpringBoot, and AWS**. While bumping into things, the process of learning one by one was a great joy for me. The logical thinking I built while designing machines on the production line shone in the code, and I grew faster than anyone else.
However, a passion for games that hadn't cooled down still remained in a corner of my heart. I, who loved games so much since childhood, became able to program, and my heart pounded at the fact that I could now make games myself. In the next post, I will tell you the story of how I couldn't control this hot passion and eventually jumped into game development.
